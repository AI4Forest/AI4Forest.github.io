French–German collaboration

AI4Forest brings together scientists from leading French and German research institutions. Their expertise spans climate science, remote sensing, artificial intelligence, mathematical optimization and forest ecology—connecting methodological innovation with the knowledge needed to understand forests at national and global scales.

  • Machine Learning
  • Remote Sensing
  • Forest Ecology
  • Climate Science
  • Optimization
  • Earth Observation
