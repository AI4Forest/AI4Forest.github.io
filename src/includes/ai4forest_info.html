AI4Forest

AI4Forest is a research project that combines artificial intelligence and forest science to better understand and manage forest ecosystems.

Research Areas
  • Climate Science
  • Remote Sensing
  • Artificial Intelligence
  • Forest Ecosystem Dynamics
  • Optimization
