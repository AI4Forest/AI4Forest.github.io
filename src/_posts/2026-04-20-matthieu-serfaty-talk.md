Dr. Matthieu Serfaty recently completed a PhD in applied mathematics at Centre Borelli, ENS Paris-Saclay. During his thesis, he worked on satellite image forensics, in particular on the detection of forged satellite images and the analysis of sensor-based traces. His work combines image processing, computer vision, statistical analysis, and sensor modeling, with a broader interest in visual content authentication and media verification.
