Welcome to the AI4Forest initiative! We are dedicated to advancing the application of artificial intelligence in forest science and management. Our goal is to bring together researchers, practitioners, and students to explore innovative solutions for forest monitoring and conservation.

# AI4Forest Summer School

We are excited to announce the AI4Forest Summer School, a unique opportunity for researchers and students to learn about the latest developments in forest AI. The summer school will feature hands-on workshops, expert lectures, and practical sessions on forest monitoring and management using artificial intelligence.

## Registration

Registration details and application process will be announced soon, find all the information at the [AI4Forest Conference & Summer School](https://ai4forest-conference.zib.de/). Stay tuned for updates!
